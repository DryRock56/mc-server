MolleFreeCam

Detach your camera and fly around to take a look, then return to exactly where you were.

What it does

"Freecam" lets you float through the world like in spectator mode, which is handy for checking a build from above or scouting a cave, without needing any client mods. When you start, a stand-in character is left at your spot so you aren't wandering off with your body.

How to use it

  1. Type /float to start.
  2. Fly around as a spectator.
  3. Type /float again to end it and return.
Time limit: freecam is on a countdown, and when it runs out you're brought back automatically. If it's too short, send a message in the Discord and I'll see what I can do.

Good to know

  • While in freecam, your stand-in stays at your spot and can be harmed, so don't go freecamming in a dangerous place.
  • Use it for checking your builds or debugging redstone, not x-raying diamonds. Don't be cheaty with, you freaking nerd!
